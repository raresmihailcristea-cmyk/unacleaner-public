Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are my photos, files, or contacts ever uploaded to a server?
No. 100% of scanning, Apple Vision photo scoring, video compression, and contact merging happen strictly offline on your own Mac, iPhone, or iPad.

Q: How do I restore my Annual Plan or Lifetime License across Mac and iPhone?
Open UnaCleaner and tap Restore Purchases on the Full Access screen while signed into the same Apple ID. StoreKit 2 syncs your entitlement automatically across macOS, iOS, and iPadOS.

Q: How do I manage or cancel an auto-renewable subscription?
Open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on iPhone/iPad or App Store → Account Settings → Subscriptions on Mac.
