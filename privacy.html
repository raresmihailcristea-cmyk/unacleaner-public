Privacy Policy for UnaCleaner

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Last Updated: November 18, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection (Data Not Collected)

UnaCleaner (for macOS, iOS, and iPadOS), developed by Rareș-Mihail Cristea, does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data.

  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: All file scans, SHA-256 duplicate checks, Apple Vision photo quality scoring, HEVC video compression, Live Photo conversions, and Contacts duplicate merging & local vCard (.vcf) backups execute strictly on your device.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: UnaCleaner embeds zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising frameworks, and zero telemetry trackers.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple StoreKit 2)

Purchases of UnaCleaner Lifetime License (com.UnaCleaner.app.lifetime) and UnaCleaner Annual Plan (com.UnaCleaner.app.sub.annual) are processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2 under the Apple Privacy Policy and Standard Apple EULA. UnaCleaner verifies cryptographically signed StoreKit 2 transaction states locally on your device.

3. App Sandbox & System Permissions

  • Photo Library: Used solely on-device to detect similar photos, screenshots, Live Photos, and large videos for user-confirmed cleanup.
  • Contacts: Used solely on-device to detect duplicate or incomplete contacts, generate local vCard backups, and merge duplicates upon confirmation.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: Used solely on-device via Apple LocalAuthentication.
  • macOS User-Selected Folders: Accessed explicitly via standard macOS open panels and local security-scoped bookmarks.

4. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

For any privacy questions (GDPR / CCPA / EU GPSR), contact Rareș-Mihail Cristea at rares.cristea@ymail.com.
